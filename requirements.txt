

1. Confirmation 1 – Futures OI Buildup
- Use Nifty Futures (NIFTY1! or current month futures from Zerodha)
- Long Buildup = Current Close > Previous Close AND Current OI > Previous OI
- Short Buildup = Current Close < Previous Close AND Current OI > Previous OI
- Automatically determine the minimum OI increase threshold (do NOT expose as a fixed user input)

2. Confirmation 2 – ATM Straddle Premium Expansion
- Auto-calculate approximate ATM strike from underlying close
- Fetch combined premium of ATM Call + ATM Put
- Confirm expansion when current combined premium is higher than the previous candle’s combined premium
- Automatically determine the minimum expansion percentage (do NOT expose as a fixed user input)

3. Confirmation 3 – Price Action
- Bullish: Candle closes above the highest high of previous N candles AND body is strong relative to the full range
- Bearish: Candle closes below the lowest low of previous N candles AND body is strong
- Automatically determine both the swing lookback length (N) and the body-strength percentage (do NOT expose as fixed user inputs)

**Automation of Parameters (Critical)**


- OI Threshold: Dynamically calculate using rolling statistics of recent OI changes (e.g., mean + 1–1.5 standard deviations, or a high percentile of the last 20–50 bars). Adapt based on current volatility regime.
- Straddle Expansion %: Auto-set using recent ATM straddle volatility / ATR of the underlying. Use a multiple of the average expansion seen in the last N bars or a volatility-adjusted percentile.
- Swing Lookback (N): Automatically choose between 3–5 (or slightly wider) based on current market regime — shorter in high volatility / strong trend, slightly longer in low volatility / range-bound conditions. Use ATR or ADX-style regime detection.
- Body Strength %: Dynamically set based on the distribution of recent candle body-to-range ratios (e.g., require the candle to be in the top 30–40% of recent body strength).
- ATM Strike: Always auto-select the nearest strike to the current futures/underlying price. No manual input.

Store historical parameter values and performance in MongoDB so the system can continuously refine the auto-thresholds over time.

**Alert Rules (Very Important)**
- Bullish Alert ONLY when ALL three are true: Long Buildup + ATM Straddle Expansion + Bullish Price Action
- Bearish Alert ONLY when ALL three are true: Short Buildup + ATM Straddle Expansion + Bearish Price Action
- Never trigger if even one confirmation is missing



**Telegram Alerts**
- When a full confirmation triggers, send a Telegram message containing:
  - Clear Bullish / Bearish signal with rating out of 10
  
  - Snapshot of the chart that includes Candlestick + Volume + OBV + TSI indicators
